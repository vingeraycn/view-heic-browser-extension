View HEIC Privacy Policy

Effective: August 14, 2026

View HEIC converts HEIC and HEIF images locally in your browser. Image contents and converted files are never sent to our analytics service.

Data we process

With “Share basic usage data” enabled, View HEIC sends coarse product events with a randomly generated pseudonymous installation ID and a short-lived session ID. Events may describe the extension version, local calendar date, feature entry point, conversion trigger, aggregate success/failure counts, coarse error category, duration, site-toggle state, and review-prompt action.

We do not collect image contents, image or page URLs, hostnames, file names, page contents, browsing history, form contents, account identifiers, advertising identifiers, precise location, or user-authored text.

Purpose and processors

We use this information only to understand feature adoption, conversion reliability, release health, and product feedback. Events first pass through a View HEIC validation proxy hosted on Cloudflare and are then processed in Google Analytics. Network providers may necessarily process connection metadata such as IP addresses under their own terms; View HEIC does not add IP addresses to its analytics payload.

Your control

Basic usage data is enabled by default. You can turn it off at any time in the extension popup. Once disabled, View HEIC stops sending events, does not cache or later replay them, and deletes the local analytics installation ID and session state. Turning it on again creates a new ID.

Retention

The current Google Analytics property retains event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for up to 14 months, with the user-retention period reset by new activity. Google may retain aggregated standard-report data for longer. The local pseudonymous ID remains on your device until you disable basic usage data or remove the extension.

Sharing, sale, and changes

We do not sell analytics data or use it for advertising. We disclose it only to the processors named above as needed to operate product analytics, or when legally required. We may revise this policy when the product or processors change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
